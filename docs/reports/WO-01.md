# WO-01 The local model on a 12 GB card: baseline — report

From the worker on the owner's computer, 2026-11-25 in the evening by that computer's clock (2026-11-26 by
UTC, which is what the download log uses).

- **Computer:** Windows 11, an NVIDIA card with 12 GB (12227 MiB by `nvidia-smi`; the picture engine sees
  12199.75 MB), 31 GB of ordinary memory.
- **App:** Lemonade Server 2026.48.3 on port 13305; its picture engine is `sd-server` (stable-diffusion.cpp).
  The log level was `debug` when the work began and was not touched.
- **Page:** this checkout, served on the computer by `test/real/run.mjs --service local`, look "on a model",
  nothing else chosen. The first runs used commit 0dc702c, the table 629b0cb, the larger model's repeat runs
  36d97c2. The words the page sends for that look did not change between them, and the same pieces gave the
  same picture, byte for byte, across all three.
- **Other programs** held between 1.1 and 2.1 GB of the card throughout. That turned out to matter (finding 2).
- **Counts:** 68 picture requests, 49 pictures made (19 different pictures; the rest are byte-for-byte
  repeats), one download of 19.3 GB. That is a few pictures over the order's "about 45".

**Tests on this branch.** `npm test`: 107 tests, 106 pass. The one that fails is in `test/progress.test.js`,
"WO-01 is in flight but its report is already here": it fails for as long as this report exists while
`docs/PROGRESS.json` still lists the order as in flight, and only the orchestrator edits that file. The privacy
test passes. The browser tests pass, 35 of 35.

## What the order's five decisions have to go on

The decisions are the orchestrator's. These are the findings each one rests on.

1. **G1, the memory step.** `--diffusion-fa` is the option a 12 GB card needs. Without it every outfit of two
   or more pieces failed, 14 times out of 14, with or without `--offload-to-cpu`. With it, alone or together
   with `--offload-to-cpu`, every outfit of two to four pieces was made, 20 times out of 20. `--offload-to-cpu`
   alone fixed nothing.
2. **G1, the one-piece failure.** With both options a one-piece outfit failed 3 times out of 6. With
   `--diffusion-fa` alone it was made 6 times out of 6. **This does not show that the option set is the cause.**
   All three failures came in the first seven minutes, while other programs held about 2.1 GB of the card; every
   one-piece run after that, under every option set, had 1.1 to 1.9 GB held by others and worked. For one piece
   the engine puts the whole model on the card as a single block and then needs about 2.45 GB of working room;
   it does this with `--diffusion-fa` alone too (same plan, same 1958 MB of working room in its log). Whether
   `--diffusion-fa` alone fails the same way when other programs hold more of the card was not measured.
3. **G2.** The saved options are where Setup looks: `recipe_options.sdcpp_args` of the model's entry in
   `GET /v1/models?show_all=true`. The value is the option string as typed. A model nobody has saved options for
   has no `sdcpp_args` key at all.
4. **G3.** The readout agrees with the app's own window once units are accounted for. The window counts in
   binary units (GiB, MiB a second) and labels them GB and MB/s; the page counts in decimal GB and megabits a
   second. Percent and time left agreed.
5. **G4.** With four pieces or fewer no piece went missing, in any picture, on either model. What goes wrong is
   faithfulness: lettering redrawn by the smaller model, an oversized shape made ordinary by both, colours
   shifted, and jewellery nobody supplied added to most outfits of two or more pieces.
6. **G5.** A 12 GB card runs `Flux-2-Klein-9B-GGUF`, but only with `--diffusion-fa`: as downloaded, with no
   options, it failed even on one piece. It takes about one and a half times as long as the smaller model. It
   kept lettering and small details clearly better and odd shapes no better.

Three things the page's author could not have known from documentation:

- **The app's `percent` is for the file being fetched, not the whole download.** At point a it read 38 while
  the whole download was at 20%. The page uses `cumulative_bytes_downloaded / total_download_size`, which is
  right.
- **There is a pause of about 30 seconds between the second and third file** during which the app reports no
  progress (seven readings at the same 18,955,210,848 bytes). The page shows a falling speed for the first
  three of them and then the size alone, with no speed and no time left, which reads as stuck.
- **The sizes shift.** The model's entry said `"size": 19.0` before the download and `"size": 18.0` after it,
  and the finished job reported 18955210848 bytes done against a total of 19291422140. Setup's list labelled
  the model "19 GB".

The test browser has no real graphics card, so Setup's question about the kind of computer showed its "not
sure" answer during this run. That says nothing about a real browser.

## 7. Verified, not verified, and the state the computer was left in

### Verified

- On this 12 GB card, `Flux-2-Klein-4B` with no options or with `--offload-to-cpu` alone makes one-piece
  outfits and fails every outfit of two, three or four pieces (14 failures of 14, 6 one-piece pictures of 6).
- With `--diffusion-fa`, alone or with `--offload-to-cpu`, it made every outfit of two, three and four pieces
  (20 of 20).
- With both options a one-piece outfit failed 3 times of 6, and the engine's log gives the reason for each
  (section 2).
- With `--diffusion-fa` alone a one-piece outfit was made 6 times of 6, while other programs held less of the
  card than during those three failures.
- Both options together are a little faster on several pieces than `--diffusion-fa` alone (29 against 35
  seconds for three, 39 against 50 for four) and leave about 3 GB less on the card between pictures.
- The saved options are in `recipe_options.sdcpp_args` of `/v1/models?show_all=true`; `/v1/health` shows the
  running ones; the two can differ.
- The download readout matches the app's window at three points, units accounted for.
- `Flux-2-Klein-9B-GGUF` runs on this card with `--diffusion-fa` and not without.
- The same pieces give the same picture every time, so pressing "try again" on a local picture changes nothing.

### Not verified

- **Whether `--diffusion-fa` alone fails on one piece when other programs hold more than about 2 GB of the
  card.** The engine's plan for one piece is the same under both option sets, so it may. This is the open
  question for G1, and it needs one-piece runs under each option set while something else holds an extra
  gigabyte or so of the card.
- Any card that is not this one. An 8 GB card, a 16 GB card and a Mac were not available.
- Whether the one-piece failure can be avoided by an engine option. None was tried beyond the two the order
  names.
- The larger model on outfits other than one and three pieces with `--diffusion-fa` alone, and more than two
  runs of any cell.
- The owner's own clothes photographed at home, a piece worn by a person, and the looks other than "on a
  model". The one cluttered photo used was a fetched shop photo of a shirt on a hanger.
- A download that is interrupted, resumed, or followed after closing the page.
- Whether a real browser on this computer suggests the right kind of computer in Setup; the test browser has
  no graphics card to recognise.
- What the last field of the app window's download row means (a "15.0 GB" after the percent).
