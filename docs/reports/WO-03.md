## A. An option that leaves room

### The candidates, and why these

The failure is that the engine plans a one-piece outfit as one segment, puts 7392.03 MB of weights on the
card, and then needs 2470.17 MB of working room beside them: 9862 MB in one block. `--max-vram` is the one
option in the help that speaks of how much of the card the engine may plan for, so all three candidates are
values of it, each beside `--diffusion-fa --offload-to-cpu`:

| candidate | why this value |
|---|---|
| `--max-vram 8` | 8 GiB (8192 MB) is less than the 9862 MB a one-piece outfit needs in one block, so the engine has to cut it; and more than the 6649 MB the last stage (the VAE decode) asks of the budget, so that stage should be left alone. |
| `--max-vram -1` | The help's "a negative value reserves that much free VRAM": keep 1 GiB of whatever is free for other programs, on any size of card. |
| `--max-vram 6` | The same idea as 8 with more left for other programs, to see what a lower budget costs. |

Not tried, and why: `--params-backend` decides where the weights wait (ordinary memory or disk), not how much
of the card is planned for, and the help says it turns `--auto-fit` off; `--disable-prefetch` only matters
once the outfit is already cut into segments; `--disable-segmented-compute` is the opposite of what is wanted;
`--vae-tiling` concerns a stage that is not the one failing.

**Lemonade refused none of them.** Each load answered `"status":"success"`, `/v1/health` showed the options as
sent (for example `--diffusion-fa --offload-to-cpu --max-vram 8`), the app's log said `Adding custom
arguments: --diffusion-fa --offload-to-cpu --max-vram 8`, and the engine's start-up lines showed
`max_vram: "8"` (`"-1"`, `"6"`).

## Verified

- Lemonade accepts `--max-vram` as a custom option and the engine runs with it (three loads, the app's answer,
  `/v1/health`, the engine's start-up lines).
- With `--diffusion-fa --offload-to-cpu --max-vram 8`, `Flux-2-Klein-4B` cuts a one-piece outfit into 27
  segments and made it 5 times in 5 with 1024 MiB held by another program, 2367 to 3064 MiB in use just
  before.
- With that option and nothing held, outfits of one, three and four pieces were all made (6 of 6) in 12 to
  39 s, the same as WO-01's table to within a second once the first run after a load is set aside (24 s
  against 21 s for that one), with peaks 9803 to 10295 MiB against WO-01's 10942 to 11377. None of its eleven
  runs has a warning from the engine about memory.
- On the published site, with that option loaded, the standard model made 5 one-piece outfits in 5 straight
  after the saved options had failed 5 in 8.
- `--max-vram -1` is worked out once at load and still plans one segment; it failed 3 of 5 with 1024 MiB held.
- `--max-vram 6` made 5 of 5 held and 6 of 6 not held, and every one of those eleven pictures had the last
  stage fail once and be redone in tiles.
- `Flux-2-Klein-9B-GGUF` with its saved options plans a one-piece outfit in 34 segments.
- With the instruction that names the added pieces, 28 one-piece pictures in 28 show whole, plain,
  full-length trousers and a plain pair of shoes, with nothing else added.
- With the owner's saved options, nothing held on purpose and the owner using the computer, a one-piece
  outfit from the standard model failed 5 times in 8 on the published site.

## Not verified

- **Any other card.** 8 was chosen from this card's 12 GB and this model's 7392 MB and 2470 MB. A card with
  less memory than the budget, or one with far more, was not tried; whether the engine accepts a budget larger
  than the card, and what it then plans, is not known.
- **Any value between 6 and 8, or above 8.** The lowest budget that leaves the last stage alone should be a
  little over 6649 MB by the engine's own figures; it was not measured.
- **`--max-vram` on `Flux-2-Klein-9B-GGUF`.** That model was run only with its saved options.
- **More than 1 GB held, or held by a real program.** The holder is a test browser with textures, and Windows
  moves its memory off the card once the engine takes the room (WO-02, and again here: the engine reported
  about 10.7 GB free during a run with the holder open). With `--max-vram 8` the peak reached 11239 MiB of
  12227 with 1 GB held.
- **Two-piece outfits, and looks other than "on a model"**, under any candidate.
- **Whether the tiled last stage under `--max-vram 6` leaves a visible seam.** One picture was opened at full
  size; the other ten were read on sheets of small copies.
- **How rare the bottoms fault now is.** 0 in 28 says it is much rarer than 4 in 11; it does not say never.
  Only two tops were used, and under one look.
- **Small faults in the added pieces.** Nearly all of the 28 one-piece pictures were read at sheet size, where
  malformed legs would show and a stitching fault would not.
- **Part B's standard-model pictures under the saved options alone.** Three of the eight are; five were made
  with the candidate loaded, as stated above.
- **What ordinary memory the candidates use.** Not measured in this order.
- **What Setup would need to know.** Whether the page's test for hiding its one more step, which looks for
  `--diffusion-fa` in the saved options, should also look for a budget is not the worker's to say; nothing
  about Setup was run in this order.

## Surprises

1. **The one-piece failure with nothing held on purpose** (part B). In WO-01 it needed about 2 GB held by
   other programs and in WO-02 a holder; here the owner's ordinary use of the computer was enough, 5 times in
   8, by margins of 95 to 197 MB. For this card and the standard model the failure is close to everyday.
2. **The larger model does not have it.** Its one-piece outfit is already cut into 34 segments under the
   saved options, so on this card the suggested model for a 12 to 16 GB card is also the one that does not
   meet this failure.
3. **A negative `--max-vram` is not live.** The help's "reserves that much free VRAM" reads as if it followed
   what is free; the engine fixes the figure when the model is loaded.
4. **`--max-vram 6` trips the last stage** into failing and being redone in tiles on every picture. A budget
   has a floor set by that stage, 6649 MB here.
5. **Two version labels.** The engine's help says `master-955-b7a74e6-2-g2988060`; the `version.txt` beside it
   says `master-957-90e86d1`.
6. **Two chest pockets** on the shirt in one four-piece picture, where the piece has one.
7. **Who is drawn changes.** With a new seed each time the person differs from picture to picture, a woman in
   most and a man in some, for the same pieces and look.
