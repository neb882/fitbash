## Not verified

- **Which word did it.** In WO-01 to WO-04 the piece was named "checkered clown shoes" and came out as
  ordinary-sized checkered lace-ups every time; here it was named "oversized clown shoes" on a later version
  of the page. Whether the difference is the word "oversized", or the page's two instruction changes since,
  was not separated: the old name was not run on this version.
- **A note without names**, and a note in other words than the order's.
- **Any odd shape but these shoes**, and any look but "on a model" for part A.
- **Why the flat lays gain trousers and shoes.** That the sentence about adding missing bottoms or shoes
  draws them is the worker's guess from the fact that what is added is plain trousers and plain shoes; no
  flat lay was made without that sentence.
- **Whether a note helps a flat lay**, and flat lays of one or two pieces.
- **The clown shoes' size in the flat lays.** Not judged and not measured: part B's five answers were the
  order's, and the shoes there have no leg above them to measure against.
- **More pictures of each kind.** Two or three of each is what the order asked for; the standard model's
  figures in particular rest on two pictures each.
- **The measure itself.** Width against the trouser leg, front on, stands in for size; a foot turned outward
  reads wider, as in the standard model's named picture 1. Length was not measured.

## Surprises

   plain beige shoes, although the shoes' picture reached it. In every earlier order the shoes had a name and
   were at least drawn, at the wrong size.
2. **The note brings its own side effects on the larger model:** a grey band under the hems in one picture,
   jewellery in another, the orange laces lost or nearly lost in two of three.
3. **Flat lays are far worse than "on a model" on both local models.** The same shirt and jeans that are worn as supplied in thirteen pictures of thirteen, with nothing bigger than
   a watch, a bracelet or a band at the hem added, come back with extra shoes or trousers in six flat lays of
   six.
4. **The standard model added a wristwatch in two of its four "on a model" pictures**, against the
   instruction's "no hats, bags, watches, jewellery".
