## Decides: three short answers

1. **Does it work from the page at all?** Not answered here. From this workspace's test browser every request
   to `api.anthropic.com` failed with `net::ERR_CERT_AUTHORITY_INVALID` before reaching Anthropic, as on
   2026-11-25. The page behaved correctly: it kept the picture and said "Not checked: The check couldn't reach
   Anthropic." The same `createCritic` code, run from Node with the real key, worked 28 times in 28, so the
   key, the model name, the request shape and the answer's shape are all good. What is still unproven is only
   the browser's own part (see Not verified).
2. **Does it catch real faults and pass good pictures?** It passed all 4 good pictures in both tries, and
   caught 8 of the 10 kinds of fault in both tries, each time naming the fault that was put in. It **missed two
   kinds in both tries**: jeans folded in half in a flat lay (D7), and a head cut off at the mouth (D10). Both
   got "pass", score 9, with every field empty.
3. **What does a check cost and how long does it take?** About 3 cents (2.98 cents for a pass, 3.2 to 3.6
   cents for a fail) and 2 to 6 seconds. Against the README's "about 4 cents a picture" for Google, the check
   adds about three quarters of a picture's cost when the picture passes; a fail followed by a redo adds
   another picture and another check. "Roughly doubles" is a fair upper description, a little pessimistic for
   the usual case.

## Costs

Price, from the `claude-api` skill's table (cached 2026-11-03): Claude Opus 5.5 is $4.00 per million input
tokens and $20.00 per million output tokens. No caching was involved (`cache_read_input_tokens` and
`cache_creation_input_tokens` were 0 in every response).

- A passing check: 6886 in, 110 out = 2.75 + 0.22 = **2.98 cents**.
- A failing check: 6886 in, 231 to 401 out = **3.2 to 3.6 cents**.
- All 28 checks: 192,428 tokens in, 7,101 out = **$0.91**; mean 3.26 cents and 3.7 seconds (range 1.8 to 5.8).
- Input tokens were the same (6886) for every 896 by 1200 picture with three 1024-pixel references; the
  cropped D10 (896 by 1022) was 6694.
- Anthropic: 28 checks used of 34. None was spent in A (nothing reached Anthropic). The first three checks in
  C cost 2.98 cents each, far under the 50-cent stop.
- Google: **16 pictures** of 22: 1 in A, 3 for G1 to G3, 1 piece, 1 for G4, 9 edits, 1 retry (D3). At the
  README's "about 4 cents a picture" that is about 64 cents; Google's price was not looked up in this session.
- Time: about 25 minutes of work; pictures took 9 to 23 seconds each.

  redraw of the same frog). The faults here were made plain on purpose.

## Surprises

- The runner's `--check-key-env` defaults to `FITBASH_TEST_CLAUDE_KEY`, so in a session where that variable is
  set every run has the check on unless told otherwise. For the good pictures I passed a variable name that
  does not exist. The order's "no check key here" needs that spelled out.
- The check is much cheaper and faster than expected: 2 to 6 seconds and about 3 cents, with little or no
  thinking at the page's `effort: "medium"` (0 thinking tokens on every pass but D7, at most 268).
- Every pass scored exactly 9 and every pass's answer but D7's was the same 110 output tokens. The score did
  not separate the good pictures from the two missed faults.
- The first D3 edit swapped the sneakers' colours (red uppers, white laces and caps) instead of only whitening
  the laces and caps; the retry, which spelled out what stays white, was right.
