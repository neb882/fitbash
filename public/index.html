fitbash

Add pictures of clothes. See them together as one outfit.

Setup

fitbash asks an AI image model to make your outfit picture. You can connect a paid service, which charges you a few cents per picture, or run a free model on your own computer if it has a good graphics card. You only do this once.

Where should pictures be made?

The quick way: sign in or create an account there and approve, and you come straight back here with the key filled in. Or do it by hand:

  1. Open the key page

  2. Get the app
  3. Press this once the app is open.

    3. Close the app, open it again, and press Connect.

Pictures made on your own computer cost nothing. They are usually less exact than a paid service's, especially on prints and lettering, and the first one takes longer while the model loads.

Optional: double-check every picture

AI pictures sometimes change a shirt's print or leave a piece out. With a Claude key, each picture is compared with your pieces and redone once if something is off. This roughly doubles the cost of each picture, billed separately by Anthropic.
